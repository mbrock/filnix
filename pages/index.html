filnix

Nix packaging for Fil-C, the memory-safe implementation of C and C++. The source is at github.com/mbrock/filnix.

  • Frame slot coloring at -O0: from cubic to near-linear
    A FilPizlonator change that takes a 160-block function from 48 s to 0.66 s at -O0 without changing any frame layout. It includes an interactive model of the old and new algorithms.
